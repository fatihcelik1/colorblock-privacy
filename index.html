Privacy Policy

App: Color Block
Developer: FCgames
Last updated: 16 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Color Block ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email or phone number) directly through the App. The App has no sign-in, no chat and no user-generated content.

The App displays advertisements through a third-party service (see section 2). To show and measure ads, that service may collect information from your device, including:

  • Device advertising identifier (Advertising ID)
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version) and diagnostic data such as crash information
  • Information about how ads were shown and interacted with

Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

2. Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may use the device Advertising ID and related information to show ads and to measure ad performance. You can reset your advertising ID or limit ad personalization in your device settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

For more information about how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy & Terms and About AdMob & data.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services - Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob - About AdMob & data

The App does not use any analytics service of its own and does not send notifications.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can remove it.

5. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your personal data. Information processed by the App is limited to what the advertising and platform services listed above require to function.

6. Your Choices

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device's privacy and ads settings.
  • Uninstall the App at any time to stop all further data processing by it.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App. We do not maintain user accounts or a server-side database of players. Data processed by the advertising service is retained according to that provider's policy. To ask a question about data associated with you, contact us at the email address below.
